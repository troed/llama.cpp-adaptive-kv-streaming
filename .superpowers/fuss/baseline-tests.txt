  action times out. Use `llama-build-bg` + wait-build.sh.
- build-tests-cuda: a first attempt died silently at 34% with no TESTS_CUDA_BUILD_EXIT
  marker while the llama router was running (host RAM was 1 GB free, load 14). After

  still not appended to build-tests-cuda.log even though the build completed; the
  artifacts (bin/*, 16:31) are fresh and the tests run, so completion was judged from
  the artifacts and the test run, not the marker.
